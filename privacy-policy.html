Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 21, 2026

Catch ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Catch mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Account Information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a username you choose. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from your Google account.

Photos. When you create a post or catch, you take a photo which is uploaded to our servers. We generate smaller versions (thumbnails) of your photos for display in the app. Photos are publicly visible to other users.

Location Data. When you create a post, we collect your device's precise GPS coordinates and compass heading to tag the location. This location data is stored securely on our servers and is never sent directly to other users' devices. Location is used to let other users navigate to the same spot and to verify catches via proximity.

Usage Data. We collect information about how you use the app, including cities you search for, posts you interact with, and users you follow. This is used to personalize your experience (e.g., recommended feed).

Device Tokens. If you enable push notifications, we store your device's push notification token to send you alerts about catches, followers, and royalties.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core app experience: posting locations, catching posts, and social features
  • To verify catch proximity (confirming you are near a post's location)
  • To display posts on the map and in feeds
  • To send you notifications about activity on your posts
  • To calculate contribution scores and royalties
  • To personalize your explore feed based on searched cities and who you follow

Optional Data Contribution

You may opt in to "Improve Catch AI" in Settings, which allows us to use your anonymized photo matches to improve our view verification model. This is entirely optional and can be toggled off at any time. Photos used for this purpose are stripped of identifying information.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) - for backend infrastructure, data storage, and push notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Mapbox - for map display and location search. Mapbox Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps (Android only) - for map rendering. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Attest / Google Play Integrity - for app verification to prevent abuse. No personal data is shared.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your username, photos, and posts are visible to other users as part of the app's social features. Your precise coordinates are never shared directly with other users; the app only confirms whether a user is within range of a location.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete a post, the associated photo and location data are removed from our servers. City search intents expire automatically after 30 days.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings > Account > Delete Account. This removes your profile, posts, photos, location data, lists, notifications, and all other data from our servers. Account deletion is irreversible.

Children's Privacy

Catch is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We implement security measures including server-side access controls, rate limiting, app attestation, and encrypted data transmission to protect your information. Location data is stored in a restricted collection that is not accessible to client devices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the app. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
